Every time you look up, you slow down.

A deck clock only helps if you break your stroke to read it: lift your head, drop your hips, and lose the back half of the lap. In a flow-current pool there's nowhere to put one at all. So most swimmers guess their pace and train in second gear.

  1. Head up, hips down. Lifting to catch the wall clock wrecks your body position and kills your streamline off every wall.
  2. You can't see it anyway. Across the pool, with goggles on, underwater, a deck clock is a blur at the moment you need your split.
  3. Nowhere to mount it. Flow-current pools and Endless Pools® leave you with no usable clock position at all.

PACE PAL® fixes all three. It sits on the bottom of the pool, in your line of sight, so you hold pace without breaking position.
